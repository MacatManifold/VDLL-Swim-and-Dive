Equipment

What to Bring · What to Buy
— Gear Up for the Season —

Everything you'll need on deck and in the water this season. Required items must be at every practice; training equipment is used for specific sets.

Daily Essentials
Required
🩱
Practice Suit
A durable training suit you can wear every day. Polyester or PBT lasts longer in chlorine than nylon/lycra.
🥽
Goggles
Bring two pairs in your bag — straps break. Tinted/mirrored for outdoor practices, clear for indoor.
🧢
Swim Cap
Silicone caps last longer than latex. Required for anyone with hair past the shoulders.
💧
Water Bottle
Large, insulated. You'll drink more than you think — bring it on deck every set.
🛍️
Mesh Equipment Bag
Lets your fins, paddles, and snorkel drain and dry between practices.
🟫
Towel(s)
Bring at least one. Two is smarter — a wet one is no fun in February.
Training Equipment
Required
🦶
Fins
Short-blade training fins (Zoomers-style). Used for kick sets and stroke work.
🟦
Kickboard
Standard foam kickboard. The team has some, but having your own is cleaner.
🟨
Pull Buoy
Foam buoy held between the thighs to isolate the upper body during pull sets.
✋
Hand Paddles
Strap-on plastic paddles for resistance and stroke awareness. Size: small to start.
🤿
Front-Mount Snorkel
Center-mounted snorkel for technique work — lets you focus on body position without breathing.
⏱️
Sport Watch (Optional)
Helpful for timing intervals on your own. Not required — pace clocks are on deck.
Meet Day
As Needed
🏆
Tech Suit
A race-day suit for championship meets. Talk to coaches before buying — they're expensive and only worn a few times per season.
👕
Team Apparel
Team t-shirt and parka for between events. Order info will go out at the start of the season.
🥪
Snacks & Food
Meets are long. Bring carbs, fruit, and electrolytes. Avoid heavy meals between events.
📓
Sharpie
For writing your event/heat/lane on your arm. Always seems to be in short supply on deck.
⚠ Before You Buy
This is a starting list — exact requirements and recommended brands will be confirmed at the team meeting. Don't rush out and buy everything before then. If cost is a concern, talk to a coach: the team has loaner equipment for athletes who need it.
